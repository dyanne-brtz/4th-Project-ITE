Competition & Christmas

A Ran & Yumi One-Shot

Ever since their first year of high school, Ran and Yumi were like cats and dogs. The way they looked at each other, you'd think they were rival gangsters.

Ran was the consistent topnotcher, arrogant, and always showing off his perfect papers. Yumi, on the other hand, was the quiet but fierce honor student who always furrowed her brows whenever she saw Ran's name on the bulletin board.

"You're really good, Ran. But you're such a teacher's pet," Yumi muttered one day while walking down the hallway.

"At least I don't neglect my studies, Yumi. Unlike some people who only care about gossip," Ran replied without even looking back.

From then on, their rivalry became all about being an achiever. If there was a quiz, they raced to submit their papers first. If there was a group project, they absolutely refused to be in the same group. They would do everything just to prove they were better than the other.

However, as their competition went on, an unexpected connection began to form.

Ran started noticing Yumi's efforts—the late nights she spent studying just to catch up to him in the rankings. Yumi, meanwhile, noticed Ran's true character—that despite his arrogance, he was always willing to help classmates who didn't understand the lessons.

One afternoon, while they were cleaning the classroom (since they were assigned to wipe down the chairs), Ran suddenly stopped. He looked at Yumi, who was busy sweeping.

"Yumi," he called out.

"What is it now, Ran? If you're going to tell me you got a higher score on yesterday's quiz, I already know. I don't care," Yumi answered without looking up.

Ran walked closer and gently grabbed the handle of her broom to stop her. "It's not that."

Yumi looked up, surprised by the serious and unusual look in his eyes.

"Sorry," Ran began. "For how I've acted towards you. Back when we started, I was just showing off. But..." He let out a deep sigh. "I realized the reason I pushed myself so hard was because I wanted us to push each other to be better. But not because of a fight, but because... I like you."

Yumi froze. The brows that always furrowed whenever she saw him suddenly relaxed. "W-what?"

"I like you, Yumi. I have for a long time," Ran replied, his cheeks visibly turning red.

Yumi didn't answer right away. But instead of getting mad or avoiding him, she smiled faintly. "Damn. You took way too long to say that."

From that day on, everything changed. Their academic rivalry turned into a partnership. They now studied under the same light in the library, helped each other with assignments, and while they used to race to submit papers, they now submitted them together. The cats and dogs were now like cats and dogs living under the same roof.

It was Christmas that year. As they walked along the edge of the campus, decorated with lights and ornaments, Ran blocked Yumi's path.

"Yumi, since we became 'us', I've been a lot happier. I hope... we can really be together."

Yumi smiled, and for the first time, she reached out her hand. "Of course, you idiot."

And under the stars and the Christmas lights, their story began—no longer as rivals, but as lovers.
