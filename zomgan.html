ZOMGAN: The Broken Halo

A ZOMGAN Story

The city of Seoul had fallen in less than forty-eight hours.

What was once a bustling metropolis was now a graveyard of burning cars, shattered glass, and the relentless, guttural moans of the undead. The military quarantine line had collapsed by dawn on the third day. Now, it was every man for himself.

In the heart of the ruined commercial district, a ragtag group of survivors took refuge inside an abandoned underground parking garage.

"Gate's sealed," a gruff voice echoed. It was Kang-ho, a former construction worker in his forties who had taken the role of the group's muscle. He wiped the sweat and grime from his forehead, his makeshift flamethrower still smoking.

"Good. We need to rest," said Sora, a young medical student who had become their unofficial medic. She was tending to a deep gash on the arm of a teenage boy named Min-jun. "We can't keep running. Min-jun needs time to heal."

Min-jun winced, clutching his bandaged arm. "I'm fine. I can keep going."

"You're not fine," Sora snapped, tying the bandage tighter. "You'll bleed out if you keep swinging that bat."

At the corner of the dark garage, leaning against a concrete pillar, was Jae-won. He was quiet, always watching, his eyes devoid of the panic that plagued the others. He held a customized handgun, twirling it slowly.

"We don't have time to rest," Jae-won said coldly, his voice cutting through the silence. "The horde from the train station is moving west. We have maybe three hours before this garage becomes a tomb."

Kang-ho grunted. "And where do you suggest we go, Jae-won? The military base is a suicide mission."

"Maybe," Jae-won replied, pushing himself off the pillar. "But it's the only place with a radio tower. If we don't call for extraction, we're just waiting to die."

The tension in the room was thick. They were a broken group of strangers brought together by tragedy. They didn't trust each other entirely, but they trusted the silence of the dead even less.

Suddenly, the heavy metal shutter of the garage rattled.

CLANG. CLANG. CLANG.

Everyone froze.

Kang-ho raised his flamethrower. Sora drew a scalpel, her hands trembling. Min-jun gripped his baseball bat. Jae-won raised his gun, his eyes narrowing.

"Not zombies," Jae-won whispered. "The rhythm is too steady."

A voice called out from the other side of the metal shutter. It was muffled, but frantic. "Please! Is there anyone in there? They're coming! Please, open the door!"

Kang-ho looked at Jae-won. "What if it's a trap? Or what if he's bitten?"

"We don't have room for a liability," Jae-won said coldly.

"Then we're no better than the monsters out there," Sora protested, stepping forward.

Before they could argue further, a loud BANG echoed from outside. A gunshot. Then, the terrifying, all-too-familiar chorus of a hundred undead throats groaning in unison. The horde had arrived.

Jae-won cursed under his breath. "Get ready. We're not just fighting for the stranger outside anymore. We're fighting to get out."

He kicked the latch of the metal shutter. It slid open with a heavy groan, revealing a terrified, bloodied young man holding an empty revolver. Behind him, coming down the sloped entrance, was a tidal wave of rotting flesh.

"Inside! Now!" Kang-ho roared.

Jae-won grabbed the stranger by the collar and threw him into the garage. He raised his gun, firing three precise shots into the skulls of the leading zombies.

"Sora! Light it up!" Jae-won commanded.

Sora threw a molotov cocktail they had crafted earlier down the ramp. The glass shattered, and a wall of fire erupted, separating them from the horde. The screams of the burning undead filled the night.

"Out the back!" Kang-ho yelled, grabbing Min-jun.

They ran through the dark tunnels, the fire roaring behind them. The new stranger stumbled, but Sora grabbed his hand and pulled him forward. They burst through an emergency exit into the open streets, the cold night air hitting their faces.

They ran until their lungs burned, the sound of the horde fading into the distance.

When they finally stopped, gasping for breath, the stranger looked at them. "I... I thought I was dead. Thank you."

Jae-won approached him, his gun still in his hand. "We didn't save you out of kindness. You owe us. You have a weapon? You fight."

The stranger nodded shakily.

Jae-won looked at the broken group—Kang-ho, Sora, Min-jun, and the stranger. They were battered, exhausted, and wounded.

"We move at dawn," Jae-won said, looking toward the horizon. "We find the military base. We survive."

It was a long road ahead. And the dead were far from finished.
