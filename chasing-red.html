Chasing Red: The Unspoken Rule

A Caleb & Veronica Story

Veronica "Red" Strafford knew exactly three things about Caleb Lockhart:

1. He was the most infuriating person she had ever met.

2. He broke every rule he could get his hands on.

3. He never, ever backed down from a challenge.

So when she found herself standing in the middle of his crowded living room during a party she didn't even want to attend, she should have known better than to open her mouth.

"You're staring," Caleb said, leaning against the doorframe of his kitchen with that stupid, arrogant smirk on his face.

Veronica crossed her arms, refusing to let him see how much his presence affected her. "I'm not staring. I'm just trying to figure out how someone can be this arrogant and still have friends."

Caleb chuckled, a low, deep sound that made her stomach flip. He pushed himself off the doorframe and walked toward her, closing the distance between them. "Maybe they're just waiting for me to screw up. Or maybe," he paused, leaning in just a little too close, "they're waiting to see what happens when I finally catch Red."

Veronica's breath hitched. She hated how easily he could do this to her. She hated how her heart raced every time he called her by that nickname. She hated how she couldn't stop thinking about him.

"You're never going to catch me, Caleb," she said, her voice barely above a whisper.

Caleb's smirk softened into something else. Something genuine. Something that made her chest ache. "I'm not trying to catch you, Red," he said quietly. "I'm just trying to keep up."

For a moment, the noise of the party faded away. It was just the two of them, standing in the middle of the chaos, and for the first time, Caleb wasn't hiding behind his arrogance.

"You confuse me," Veronica admitted, her guard dropping.

"Good," Caleb said, reaching up to tuck a stray strand of red hair behind her ear. "That means I'm doing something right."

Veronica didn't pull away. Instead, she looked up at him, her blue eyes meeting his dark ones. "What are you really after, Caleb?"

He held her gaze, his hand lingering near her cheek. "I think you know."

She did know. And that terrified her more than anything. But before she could overthink it, Caleb leaned down and pressed his lips to hers, soft and slow, like he was afraid she might disappear. Veronica froze, but only for a second. Then, she kissed him back.

When they pulled apart, Caleb rested his forehead against hers. "You know," he murmured, "red is my favorite color."

Veronica let out a breathless laugh, her hands resting on his chest. "Shut up, Caleb."

He grinned—a real, genuine grin. "Make me."

And for the first time in a long time, Veronica didn't want to run away. She had spent so long running, but Caleb had finally caught her. And she realized, maybe, she wanted to be caught all along.
